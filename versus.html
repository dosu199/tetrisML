How to play

  • ← → move
  • ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate the other way
  • ↓ drop faster, Space drop at once
  • C or Shift hold
  • P or Esc pause

Battle: clearing 2 lines at once sends 1 garbage row to the other board, 3 lines send 2, and a tetris (4 lines) sends 4. Clearing lines also cancels garbage that is coming your way. Whoever fills up to the top first loses. Race: first to 40 lines wins; topping out loses. Both of you get exactly the same pieces and can see the next one and use hold. The network decides at the speed you choose; set it low to warm up.
