Play vs Bot

You and the network get exactly the same pieces. Pick a match, then press Start.

How to play
  • ← → move
  • ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate the other way
  • ↓ drop faster, Space drop at once
  • C or Shift hold, P or Esc pause

Use the buttons under the boards: ◀ ▶ move, ⟲ ⟳ rotate, ▼ faster, Drop, Hold.

Battle: clearing 2 lines at once sends 1 garbage row to the other board, 3 lines send 2, and a Tetris (4 lines) sends 4. The red bar beside a board shows garbage on its way; your own clears cancel it. Whoever fills up to the top first loses. Race: first to 40 lines wins; topping out loses.
